§ 3. The Unshakable Citadel

Men look for retreats for themselves, the country cottages, shores, and mountains. You too are especially wont to yearn for such things. But this is the work of an ordinary mind, when at what hour soever you please retreat into yourself.

For nowhere does a man retire with more quietness or freedom than into his own soul; especially if he has within him thoughts of such a nature that by looking into them he immediately finds himself in complete contentment: and by contentment I mean nothing other than well-ordered composure.

Constantly give yourself this retreat, and renew yourself. Let your principles be brief and fundamental, which as soon as you have recurred to them will be sufficient to cleanse the soul and send you back without any irritation to the life whereto you return.
